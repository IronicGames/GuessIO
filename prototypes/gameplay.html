UX Notes — Gameplay Prototype

Timers in the Header

Turn timer and game timer sit in the header centre alongside the join code. They're always visible regardless of where you're looking, and the header is the natural "always-on" zone. Turn timer changes colour: cyan → yellow (under 30s) → red pulse (under 10s).

Status Bar Above the Board

A thin bar directly above the board shows turn number, phase description, and a turn badge ("Your Turn / Opponent's Turn"). This is the first thing you see when looking at the board and removes any ambiguity about whose turn it is or what you're supposed to do.

Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3

Ask / Guess are large click targets in the action area (Phase 1). Choosing one commits you to that path. In Phase 2 the action area updates to show what you've selected and enables the Confirm button. Phase 3 shows End Turn — cross-offs happen on the board itself.

Cross-Offs vs Lobby Disabled

Crossed-out cards use an X overlay made of two CSS lines + reduced opacity — intentionally different from the lobby's grayscale(40%) disabled state. Crossed out means "I've eliminated this one" (reversible, local only). Disabled in the lobby means permanently removed from the game pool.

Tags Panel — Collapsible Left Strip

The tags panel is collapsible to a 36px strip so the board gets maximum space. In Phase 2 (asking, Tag Mode) tags become interactive — click a tag to select it, which populates the action area and enables Confirm. Outside of that phase, tags are read-only reference.

"i" Button on Character Cards

A small circular button in the corner of each card. Clicking it (without triggering the card's cross-off) filters the tag list to only that character's tags. An "× Show all" link in the tag panel header clears the filter. Useful for "does Gengar count as Ghost for my question?"

Guessing Flow

After clicking Guess (Phase 1), cards enter guess-select mode — hovering shows a subtle selection affordance. Clicking a card highlights it in cyan (selected-guess state) and populates the action area. The character's colour is preserved so you're reminded who you're committing to.

Your Secret Character (Gold Border)

Your chosen character is marked with a gold border on the board throughout the game. It can't be crossed off (it's your character). It's also shown as a small thumbnail in the Game Info section. The opponent cannot see this — it's purely client-side UI.

Opponent's Turn

During the opponent's turn the action area shows a spinner and "PixelFox is thinking…". The board stays visible and cross-offs are still enabled — you can keep tidying up while waiting. This is different from blocking the whole UI.

Character Selection Phase

Shown before Turn 1. The action area prompts you to click a character. A "Random" button is available. The turn timer runs during this phase — if it expires, a random character is assigned. Once confirmed, your card gets the gold border.

Game Log Tab

The right panel bottom section has Chat and Game Log tabs. Game Log shows automated per-action entries. In Tag Mode it includes the tag asked and the yes/no answer. In Casual Mode it just says "asked a question". Useful for reviewing what's been asked without scrolling back through chat.

Casual Mode Differences

In Casual Mode: tags panel is hidden (no tags exist on custom boards), turn timer is always off, Phase 2 asking shows "ask your question externally" + End Turn immediately. The game is honour-based — the UI just tracks turns and guesses.
